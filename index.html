About

One consultant. Every engagement. No hand-offs.

The work that actually moves a company — positioning, voice, operating structure — gets diluted the moment it passes through layers. So it doesn't. Every client works directly with the principal, from the first call to the final handoff.

Agency-side, then operator-side, then here. That's the only order that makes sense. Six engagements per year keeps it that way.

Weeks 5–8Build. First real output ships at week six. Not a draft — a decision.
Weeks 9–12Rollout and handoff. We leave with something running. Not a deck explaining what someone else should do.

The old site read like every premium outdoor brand: polished, thoughtful, and impossible to distinguish from the rest of the category.

A week in the warehouse made it clear that people were not buying gear, they were buying permission to take themselves seriously outside. We rewrote the voice around that tension. The about page became 47 words. The returns page became six.
